Fly through
your color.

You're a dart in three lanes. Walls come at you fast. One section of each wall is your color: that's the one you go through. That's the whole game, and it gets wild.

You never shoot a boss.
You outsmart it.

There's no fire button. Every boss is beaten with its own tricks: fly its wall of flame without touching it and the fire rushes back. Lead the sea monster's lure away and its jaws snap shut on nothing. Play the Conductor's tune back to it. Tow a black hole under a star.

Each of the 16 worlds ends in one, and no two are fought the same way.

Walls that cheat.

Walls slide sideways at the last second. They swap, mirror, lock, count down and spin. Portals throw you into another lane. Ice makes you slide. A new world means a new trick, and the first level always shows it to you slowly before it's used against you.

Short levels. One more go.

A level is about a minute: a run of walls, then a fight. Lose and you're back in within a second. When the worlds are done there's Endless, which shuffles them together and hands you power-ups as you go, and three new Daily Challenges every morning.

Fly a dragon. Or a turtle
with a frisbee.

There are dozens of skins and each one is its own little creature or ship, with its own way of moving. Coins from playing buy them, and upgrade your ship in the Hangar: extra lives, shields, and Power Cores that slow time or let you phase through a wall.
