Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Color Match ("the game", "we") collects and how it is used. In short: the game has no accounts and we don't collect your personal information. Ads, when you choose to watch one, are provided by Google AdMob, described below.

Information stored on your device

Your progress, coins, unlocked items and settings are saved on your device. They are not sent to us. On iPhone and iPad, if iCloud is turned on for the game, a copy of your progress is also kept in your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud storage), so it comes back if you reinstall the game or move to a new device. We cannot see or access it. You can turn this off in your device's iCloud settings. Deleting the game deletes the copy on your device.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple (the App Store). We never see or store your payment details. Apple tells the game only which item was bought, so it can be unlocked and restored on your other devices.

Advertising

The game shows ads only when you choose to watch one for a reward (for example a revive or double coins). There are no forced ads. Buying Remove Ads gives every reward without watching an ad.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address (used for approximate location), device type and how you interact with ads. On iPhone, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking when the game asks; otherwise they are not personalized. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Players in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent first. AdMob is set to show family-safe ads only. Google's own policy explains how it uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, accounts or logins.
  • We don't collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We don't sell your information.
  • No chat or contact with other players.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone but is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are limited to family-safe content.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
