Questions, bugs or ideas? Email supportcolormatch@gmail.com. Please include your device (for example iPhone 15) and the build number shown small on the game's home screen.

Common questions

I bought something and got a new phone (or reinstalled the game).

Open Settings > Purchases > Restore Purchases. Skins, bundles and Remove Ads come back on any device signed in to the same Apple ID. Coin packs are used up when bought, so they can't be restored.

A purchase went through but I didn't get it.

Try Restore Purchases first. If it's still missing, email us with the date of the purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The game feels warm or uses a lot of battery.

Set Settings > Frame Rate to COOLER. The game then runs at about 60 frames a second instead of 120.

Can I play with a color vision deficiency?

Yes: every color also has its own symbol (Settings), and every warning is a shape and a sound, not only a color.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game plays fully offline. Only purchases and optional reward ads need a connection.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 16 or newer (iPhone 8 and newer).
