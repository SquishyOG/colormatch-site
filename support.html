Reporting a bug?

Three things help me find it fast.

  1. Your deviceLike “iPhone 13” or “iPad Air”.
  2. The build numberThe small text at the top of the game's home screen.
  3. What happened, and whereThe world and level, or the screen you were on. A screenshot or a quick screen recording is gold.

Quick fixes

I got a new phone or reinstalled the game. Where are my purchases?

Open Settings › More › Restore Purchases (it's also at the bottom of the Store's Coins tab). Skins, bundles and Remove Ads come back on any device signed in to the same Apple ID. Coin packs are spent when you buy them, so those can't come back.

I paid for something but didn't get it.

Try Restore Purchases first. Still missing? Email me with the date you bought it and I'll sort it out. Apple handles refunds directly at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My phone gets warm, or the battery drains fast.

Go to Settings › Graphics › Frame Rate and pick Cooler. The game runs at about 60 frames a second instead of 120 and your phone stays cooler.

Can I play if I'm color blind?

Yes. Turn on Color Symbols in Settings and every color gets its own shape. Every warning in the game is also a shape and a sound, never just a color.

Do I need to be online?

No, the whole game plays offline. You only need a connection to buy something or to watch an optional ad for a reward.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 16 or newer (iPhone 8 and up).

A boss is too hard. Any tips?

Every boss is hurt its own way, usually by turning its own attack back on it. Watch for the glowing signs: a box with arrows means “be here”, teeth and stripes mean danger. Settings › More shows all the boss signs, and Boss Hints (Settings › Gameplay) can give you more help.
